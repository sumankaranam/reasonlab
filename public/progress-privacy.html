Your cloud progress choices

Learning works without cloud saving. It is off by default and an adult chooses whether to enable it on each device.

What is saved

When enabled, ReasonLab stores your learner nicknames and chosen avatars, selected grade settings, completed Kids and Reasoning Adventures summaries, French/Tamil/Kannada lesson progress and vocabulary review schedules. This copy is linked to your adult account identifier in Cloudflare's database.

Cloud saving does not include guest work, personality or interest reflections, Classic history, recordings, unfinished answers or unfinished lesson checkpoints. Use a nickname rather than a child's full name. Google and Supabase continue to handle adult sign-in; the cloud save does not contain your sign-in tokens.

Another device

Sign in to the same adult account, open Adult account and enable cloud saving. If a saved copy exists, choose whether to restore it or replace it with this device's progress. Restoring replaces the included local progress and can reset unfinished language activities. It does not import guest work.

Completed changes are saved while an enabled learning page is open and connected. Wait for the saved confirmation or use Save / check cloud before closing. Offline changes remain on the device. When two copies differ, automatic saving pauses for the adult to choose which to keep; it does not merge them silently.

Control and deletion

Turn off on this device stops its cloud saving and leaves device and cloud copies in place. Delete cloud copy removes the stored profiles and progress, but keeps device copies and the adult sign-in account. A minimal account identifier and revision marker remains to prevent another device from silently recreating deleted progress. An adult can explicitly upload a device copy again.

Cloud progress is kept until an adult deletes it. It is separate from aggregate visit statistics, which use a different retention policy. Local copies are not encrypted by ReasonLab; sign out on shared devices and use your device's security controls. Cloud saving is not end-to-end encryption.
