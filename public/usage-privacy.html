How visit statistics work

We count page loads and estimated tab visits to understand whether ReasonLab is being used. Our statistics database stores only daily totals for the activity hub, Kids, French, Discovery, Account and Classic. Totals are retained for about 90 days.

These counts do not include child names, account IDs, email addresses, answers, audio, IP addresses or individual visit records. Cloudflare still processes network request information to serve the website. Daily owner emails contain aggregate totals and adult registration counts from Supabase.

A timestamp in this tab’s session storage distinguishes a new visit from another page load within 30 minutes. It is not a visitor identifier and is not sent to the server. Visits are estimates, not unique people; multiple tabs and devices can count separately.

We also respect Global Privacy Control and the browser’s Do Not Track signal. Blocking statistics does not limit learning. This choice is stored on this device and takes effect on future page loads.

This page explains visit statistics only. Adult sign-in and optional speech recognition have separate data handling described in the app.
